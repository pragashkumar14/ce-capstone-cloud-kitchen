Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name and pickup time, which are stored in our database to fulfil your order.

If you optionally provide your address to get directions, it is used only in your browser session to generate a Google Maps link and is never saved to our database.

Why we collect it

Your name and pickup time are necessary to prepare and identify your order for collection. This is the lawful basis of contractual necessity — we cannot fulfil an order without knowing who it belongs to.

How long we keep it

Order records are currently retained indefinitely. This project is an academic capstone demonstration; a production deployment would define and enforce a specific retention period (for example, deleting orders after 90 days).

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your name or order details with any third party. If you request directions, your address is sent directly to Google Maps by your own browser — it never passes through our servers.

How it's protected

Your data is stored in an encrypted database (AWS RDS, encryption at rest enabled) that is not publicly accessible from the internet, and is only reachable by our application servers.

Your rights

As this is a student project, there is currently no self-service way to request deletion of your data. If you have concerns, please contact us using the details on the site.
